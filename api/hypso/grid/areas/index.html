hypso.grid.areas#

pyresample geometry for hypso products: the swath a product was imaged in, the grid a regridded product is on, and new grids to regrid onto.

Grids from grid_area() have their extent snapped to whole multiples of the pixel size, so two captures regridded to the same CRS and resolution share pixel boundaries and can be compared pixel by pixel.
