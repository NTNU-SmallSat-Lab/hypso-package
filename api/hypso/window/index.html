hypso.window#

Pixel windows: a product’s pixels in a size x size box around a site and their per-band statistics (Product.window).

This is the product side of a matchup only. Ground truth, time matching, other sensors, QC thresholds and the matchup protocol itself belong to the processing pipeline (hypso-processing-pipeline), which calls this.

The statistics are those of the pipeline’s extraction.py for HYPSO: find the nearest pixel, take the box clipped at the image edges, NaN-aware mean/median/std per band, CV = std / median, cv_median over a band range (default 405-570 nm, the pipeline’s convention), a pixel counted valid when any band in that range is finite. No outlier rejection: per the usual matchup protocol (Bailey & Werdell 2006) heterogeneity shows in the CV instead. Differences: flagged pixels can be excluded (exclude_flags, pixel_flags names), and the mean angles are the window’s, not the whole scene’s. The nearest pixel is found geodesically (Product.pixel), not by Euclidean distance in degrees as the pipeline does.
