.. py:class:: SpectralResponse

   SRFs and what is derived from them. Shapes: n_bands = binned band
   count, n_unbinned = pre-binning band count, n_grid = wavelength grid.


.. py:function:: band_irradiance(srfs, ssi)

   Per-band solar irradiance F0: the irradiance averaged with
   each band's SRF as weights.


.. py:function:: bin_srfs(srfs, bin_factor)

   Sum each group of bin_factor neighbouring SRFs and renormalise to a
   peak of 1 (extra bands beyond a whole number of bins are dropped).


.. py:function:: compute_spectral_response(band_centers_unbinned, fwhm_unbinned, bin_factor, ssi_source = 'tsis')

   Spectral response of a sensor's bands.

   :param band_centers_unbinned: Band centres and FWHM in nm before spectral binning.
   :type band_centers_unbinned: array_like
   :param fwhm_unbinned: Band centres and FWHM in nm before spectral binning.
   :type fwhm_unbinned: array_like
   :param bin_factor: Spectral binning factor of the image.
   :type bin_factor: int
   :param ssi_source: Solar irradiance spectrum for the per-band F0.
   :type ssi_source: {"tsis", "thuillier"}, default "tsis"

   :rtype: SpectralResponse


.. py:function:: earth_sun_distance(iso_time)

   Earth-Sun distance in AU for a date.

   ``1 - 0.01672 cos(0.9856 (doy - 4))`` with the cosine argument in degrees:
   0.9856 deg/day is the mean orbital motion, day 4 is perihelion. (Until
   2026-10-23 the argument was passed as radians, a date-dependent TOA
   reflectance error of up to about 6%.)

   :param iso_time: Acquisition time.
   :type iso_time: str or datetime

   :returns: Distance over the mean distance (R / R_0).
   :rtype: float


.. py:function:: effective_fwhm(srfs, wavelengths)

   Width (nm) of each SRF at half its maximum.


.. py:function:: gaussian_srfs(ssi, solar_wavelengths, sensor_wavelengths, sensor_fwhm)

   One Gaussian SRF (peak 1, +/- :data:`SRF_N_SIGMA` sigma) per band on the irradiance
   spectrum's wavelength grid, truncated to the first band's start and the
   last band's end. Returns (srfs as sparse CSR, truncated irradiance,
   truncated wavelengths).


.. py:function:: load_solar_irradiance(source = 'tsis')

   A reference solar irradiance spectrum.

   :param source: TSIS-1 HSRS v2 (2022-11-30, 0.005 nm sampling) or Thuillier (2002).
   :type source: {"tsis", "thuillier"}, default "tsis"

   :returns: * **irradiance** (*numpy.ndarray*) -- In mW m-2 nm-1.
             * **wavelengths** (*numpy.ndarray*) -- In nm.


.. py:function:: srf_dataset(sr, wavelengths, fwhm, iso_time=None, band_indices=None)

   A product's ``metadata/srf`` group.

   :param sr:
   :type sr: SpectralResponse
   :param wavelengths: The product's band centres and FWHM (nm).
   :type wavelengths: array_like
   :param fwhm: The product's band centres and FWHM (nm).
   :type fwhm: array_like
   :param iso_time: Acquisition time, for ``earth_sun_distance_au``.
   :type iso_time: str, optional
   :param band_indices: Each band's index in ``sr`` (the sensor's full band set), for a
                        product with a subset of bands (``Product.select_bands``); default
                        ``0..n-1``. The per-band tables and ``Band_<index>`` SRFs follow it.
   :type band_indices: array_like of int, optional

   :returns: ``F0``, ``F0_wavelengths``, ``effective_fwhm(_unbinned)``, and one
             ``Band_<i>`` variable per band (the non-zero part of its binned SRF,
             on its own ``wav_Band_<i>`` coordinate). Polymer reads its SRFs from
             here (:func:`hypso.ac.ac_polymer.ac_polymer_srf_getter_from_l1`).
   :rtype: xarray.Dataset
